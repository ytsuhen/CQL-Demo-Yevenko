This audit trace comes from a live demo of clinical rules written in CQL over FHIR, with a language model at the edges. It shows what was received from a synthetic patient, what the language model read from it, what the rule engine asked and decided, and where the result was sent.

Nothing is stored on a server: the whole trace is in the link you opened. The same inputs and the same rule version always give the same decision and the same number.
